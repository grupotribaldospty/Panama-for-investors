New to Panama? The city sits on the Pacific Ocean, at the entrance to the Canal. The international airport (Tocumen) is to the east, 15–35 min from the residential areas. The most sought-after areas line the bay. The beaches are 1–2 h west; Boquete is a 1 h flight.
